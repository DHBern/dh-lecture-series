# Welcome to the DH Lecture Series, Autumn 2026

Welcome to this year's _Ringvorlesung_ of the Digital Humanities at the University of Bern. In
this post we present the programme for the autumn semester 2026.

Speakers from history, linguistics, art history and digital humanities offer insights into how
digital methods are currently being theorised and put to work — and into what they imagine the
digital humanities will become. This year's contributions range from writing processes in
mobile messaging to medieval women's manuscript production, from measurement uncertainty in
computational narratology to visual language models.

**Mondays, 14:15–16:00, Seminarraum F-122, Hörraumgebäude Unitobler, Lerchenweg 36.** Several
dates are followed by a **workshop from 16:15 to 18:00**; attendance at the workshops is
expected of enrolled students, and everyone else is welcome to join.

The series is held **in German and English** — each talk is given in the speaker's own
language. On the course website every abstract appears in its original language with a
translation into the other.
